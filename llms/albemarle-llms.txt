# Albemarle Corporation

> Albemarle Corporation (NYSE: ALB) is a global leader in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of lithium and bromine-based specialty chemicals. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company transforms essential resources into critical ingredients for mobility, energy, connectivity, and health. Albemarle operates approximately 23 locations worldwide, employs roughly 7,800 people, holds over 1,500 active patents, and serves customers in approximately 70 countries.

Albemarle's two core business segments are Energy Storage (lithium compounds for EV batteries, grid storage, consumer electronics, and specialty glass) and Specialties (bromine-based flame retardants, fire safety compounds, pharmaceutical precursors, water treatment chemicals, and specialty lithium solutions). As of early 2026, the company has divested a majority stake in its former Ketjen catalyst business.

Albemarle extracts lithium from brine operations in Chile and Nevada and hard-rock spodumene in Australia, then converts these into lithium carbonate, lithium hydroxide, and lithium chloride. In bromine, the company holds a leading global market position alongside ICL Group, sourcing from operations in Arkansas and Jordan.

Key industries served include automotive and EV, grid-scale energy storage, aviation and aerospace, electrical and electronics, construction, pharmaceutical and nutrition, oil and gas, and water treatment.
